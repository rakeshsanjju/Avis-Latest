About Avis Hospital

Winner of Times Network’s “All India Best Varicose Veins Hospital” Award.

Avis Hospitals are India’s first and only chain of Vascular and Interventional Radiology Hospitals. Established by Dr. Rajah V Koppala in 2015, we provide the same quality of varicose vein treatments as the world’s leading institutes.

Minimally invasive treatments are at the core of our treatment approach. We believe that any treatment should be quick, affordable and effective.

  • Fast Treatments.
  • No Hospitalisation.
  • Little to No Pain.
